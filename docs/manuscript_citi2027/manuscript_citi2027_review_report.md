## Overall assessment

The manuscript makes a defensible systems-and-evaluation contribution: it demonstrates that a frozen adversarial gate changes four of nine decisions accepted by a clean-only criterion. The strongest evidence is the fully crossed three-dataset, three-seed evaluation and exact decision traceability. The main limitations are feature-space rather than packet-space realization, one MLP family, three seeds, fixed datasets, Edge scenario confounding, and policy dependence. These limitations are visible and appropriately constrain the conclusion. No claim of guaranteed security, “first,” state of the art, or acceptance likelihood remains.
