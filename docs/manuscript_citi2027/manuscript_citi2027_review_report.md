## Overall assessment

The revised manuscript makes a defensible systems-and-evaluation contribution: a validation-derived adversarial gate changes four of nine decisions accepted by a clean-only criterion (44.4%). Its strongest evidence is the fully crossed three-dataset, three-seed evaluation, uncertainty-aware reporting, exact policy-digest consistency, and decision traceability. Its main limitations are feature-space rather than packet-space realization, one MLP family, three seeds, fixed datasets, Edge scenario confounding, policy dependence, and the absence of prospective policy preregistration before TEST attack generation. The discussion distinguishes observation, interpretation, and causal scope; all limitations constrain the conclusion. No claim of guaranteed security, “first,” state of the art, universal thresholds, chronologically blind policy selection, or acceptance likelihood remains.
