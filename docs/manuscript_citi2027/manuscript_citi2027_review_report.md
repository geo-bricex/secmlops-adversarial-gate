## Overall assessment

The revised manuscript makes a defensible systems-and-evaluation contribution: a frozen adversarial gate changes four of nine decisions accepted by a clean-only criterion (44.4%). Its strongest evidence is the fully crossed three-dataset, three-seed evaluation, uncertainty-aware reporting, and exact decision traceability. Its main limitations are feature-space rather than packet-space realization, one MLP family, three seeds, fixed datasets, Edge scenario confounding, and policy dependence. The discussion now distinguishes observation, interpretation, and causal scope; all limitations constrain the conclusion. No claim of guaranteed security, “first,” state of the art, universal thresholds, or acceptance likelihood remains.
